Plot Synopsis

The financially struggling female protagonist seized an opportunity to turn her life around one night, completely unaware that criminals behind the scenes were attempting to use this chance to do something malicious. In the end, the protagonist successfully grasped the ticket that could lift her from rock bottom, ready to board her starlight flight and realize her dream.

Description

We made the environments for the animation to be highly detailed, while striking a perfect balance between not being too empty and not too crowded with unimportant objects. The main element we used to increase the beauty of the scenes in our animation, is the use of warm and cold lighting and using high quality texture files for the objects on the scene. Overall, we learnt a lot and made improvements as time goes by during production of this animation, such as going for a more "grand/high quality" feel for the environments compared to what these environments would've look like in real life
